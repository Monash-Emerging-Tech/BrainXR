# DirectionalVectors

Overlays projected directional gaze vector rays, coordinate reticles, and field-of-view HUD readouts centered over the 3D VR Headset on the brain during Beat 3.

## Inputs & Props
- `opacity: number`: 0 to 1 scalar controlling the overall visibility and fade of the directional vectors.

## Behavior
Visualizes how BrainXR captures the directional component of the focus vector variable via XR head orientation and spatial gaze tracking.
